What to show the inspector

Note which rooms you use for sleeping and where you found live bugs, shed skins, or dark spots. An inspector can check mattress seams, the headboard, the bed frame, and nearby upholstered furniture. Ask what evidence they found and which rooms it affects.

Who to contact if you rent

Tell your property manager where and when you noticed the signs. Ask who will schedule the inspection, whether nearby units need to be checked, and what to do with bedding or furniture before the visit.

What to ask before booking treatment

Ask the provider why they recommend a particular method, which rooms they will treat, how to prepare, and when people and pets can return. Request a written estimate and ask whether it includes a follow-up inspection. If they recommend heat, ask how they will monitor temperatures.
